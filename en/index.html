Privacy

FREE + PAID · ANDROID
01

Game data on your device

Save files, gameplay statistics and settings are stored locally in Android app storage so your progress and preferences remain available. The apps do not require a player account. These gameplay data are not sent to AppsMakerDeluxe.

02

Backups and save import

You can export a backup from GlacierFort: Prologue through Android’s system file picker. In GlacierFort: Last Refuge, you can select and import a file. The backup is saved at the location you choose and may contain your personal game progress. Keep it secure and delete it from that location when you no longer need it.

03

Internet, ads and analytics

GlacierFort is ad-free and playable offline. The apps contain no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. You do not need a player account or an internet connection to play. Your app store processes data under its own terms, such as when you download or purchase an app.

04

Permissions and background activity

The apps do not access your location, contacts, camera or microphone. You start file imports and exports through Android’s system file picker. The game does not send saves to a cloud service or continue running after it is closed.

05

Deleting data and retention

Save files, statistics and settings remain in local app storage until you clear the app data or uninstall it. Files you export remain at the destination you selected, where you can delete them. Information in email enquiries is used to reply. Please do not email passwords or private save files.

06

Website and contact

This static GitHub Pages site loads no analytics or advertising scripts, sets no first-party tracking cookies and loads no external fonts. GitHub processes technical connection and site-use data under its own privacy statement. Your email provider transmits messages you send. Privacy enquiries: AppsMakerDeluxe Studios, appsmakerdeluxe@gmail.com.
